# Westbay: the city's canon

The city next to the town (owner, 2026-11-25: "mở rộng thêm khu bên cạnh dạng như thị xã thành phố … nhớ đảm bảo
context các nhân vật có thể quen nhau từ trước, đừng xây dựng mọi thứ một cách hời hợt"). The design the owner approved
is `docs/city-proposal.md`; how it is built is `docs/town.md` §20. **This file is the canon**: who everybody is, who
knows whom, what has happened and what has not. Every line written for the city must agree with it and with the town's
own lines (`src/data/townStories.ts`, `src/data/stories/*.ts`, `src/data/streetTalks/`, `src/data/scenes/`).

## 1. The place

- **Westbay**, a small city on a bay, forty minutes from the town by **bus no. 9** (from Sam's bus station; the last
  bus back is at 10:15 pm, as Sam says in the town). People in the town call it "the city".
- Three districts are open:
  - **the station** (Central Station, the terminal plaza where the no. 9 stops, the taxi rank, Tess's coffee cart);
  - **the old market** (Market Street's shops and the market square with its fountain);
  - **Arts Street** (the Lumen cinema, the Harbour Press bookshop, the Bay FM studio, Bo's karaoke bar).
  The seafront promenade runs along the bay. Later districts, not open and not to be visited in a line yet: the office
  district, the university and Westbay Hospital, the harbour with the ferry and the night market. They may be
  *mentioned* ("up at the hospital", "the ferry").
- **Bay FM** is the city's radio; its presenter **Marcus** is heard on the radio in the town's café and at Gianna's.

## 2. The six main people (three episodes each)

Each is tied to a town resident by something the town's lines already say. The town resident's place id is in
brackets; a town resident's lines in the city are in their own voice.

### Rico, taxi driver at the station rank (Sam's cousin) [station]
- Sam in the town: "My cousin drives one [a taxi]. Want me to give him a call?" Rico is that cousin, a few years older.
- Talks ten times as much as Sam. Knows every street, every shortcut and everybody's business. Warm, a show-off, kind.
- Sam hates phone calls; he texts. It is Rico who phones Sam's mum (Rico's aunt, **Auntie Rosa**) "for him".
- Wants to start a proper dispatch office one day instead of waiting at the rank.
- Episodes:
  1. **The learner's first minutes in Westbay.** Rico spots them off the no. 9: Sam texted him ("He never calls. He
     texts. Three words."). He explains the city in a breath: the old market up Station Road, Arts Street down towards
     the bay, and sends them to the florist's on Market Street, "tell Nora that Rico sent you". Mrs Okafor calls from
     the ticket window that his taxi is blocking the rank again.
  2. **The phone call.** Sam is in Westbay. Rico puts Auntie Rosa on speaker and makes Sam talk to his own mother. Sam
     manages a few short lines; she is delighted (her voice is never heard: we hear the two cousins). Funny, then
     tender: Sam admits he never knows what to say; Rico: "You say hello. That's the whole trick."
  3. **End of a long shift**, at Tess's coffee cart. Why Rico talks so much: twelve hours alone in a car. A passenger
     he still thinks about. The dispatch office he wants: two phones, a map on the wall.

### Nora, florist, Sunflower Corner on Market Street (Leo's older sister) [square]
- In the town Leo has a sister in the city who wants him to move back. Nora is that sister. She has just had a baby
  girl, **Maya** (three months). Her husband is **Theo**, a ferry mechanic, quiet, on leave with the baby.
- The shop is named for the sunflowers their mum grew on the balcony of the flat they grew up in.
- She misses Leo and half understands why he stays: the town needs him (the square, the board-game nights).
- Episodes:
  1. Rico sent the learner. Nora finds out they know Leo and asks how he *really* is ("He says 'fine'. He always says
     'fine'."). Maya is asleep in the pram outside; Theo is minding her.
  2. **Leo visits.** Sister and brother: she wants him back in the city, he has a town that needs him. Nobody wins.
     Maya has his curls. He holds her for the first time without being told how.
  3. Nora and Theo after closing. The balcony sunflowers. She decides to stop asking Leo to move and to bring Maya to
     the town's lantern festival instead.

### Frank, second-hand comics stall on Market Street (Ben's dad) [shop]
- Ben in the town: "My dad opened this place thirty years ago." "My dad's coming by the shop on Saturday." "Thirty
  years, and he still checks every shelf." "My dad says I'm the worst joke teller alive." Frank is that dad.
- Retired from the shop, sells second-hand comics from a stall because he cannot sit still. Proud of the shop, knows
  times have changed.
- A secret: years ago the supermarket chain **FreshWay** offered to buy the shop. He said no and never told Ben. Its
  area manager now is **Colin**, who stacked shelves for Frank as a boy of fifteen.
- Episodes:
  1. Frank learns the learner shops at Ben's. Is the bell over the door still sticking? Does Ben still tell that joke?
     Hal from the fish stall teases him: he asks every customer from the town the same questions.
  2. **Ben visits** and finds a letter from FreshWay on the stall. He thinks his dad doubts him. Frank: he refused
     them before Ben could walk the aisles alone; the letter is a new one, and the answer is the same.
  3. Frank and Poppy from the bakery. He will bring the stall to the town's Saturday market. Colin keeps ringing;
     Frank remembers him at fifteen, eating more crisps than he stacked.

### Gianna, owner of the Italian restaurant on Market Street [cafe]
- Emma in the town: "It's my birthday on Saturday! Dinner at that Italian place in the city." Emma's grandma "had a
  tiny cafe, just like this one"; Emma has "my grandma's recipe book. Nobody's seen it but me", and makes her
  grandma's lemon cake.
- Gianna came to Westbay as a student, poor and homesick, and ate at Emma's grandmother's café most days. The old
  lady fed her and wrote out the lemon cake recipe for her on a card. Gianna still has the card, in the grandmother's
  handwriting. Emma does not know this until episode 2.
- Loud, generous, feeds everybody, never sits down. Has not closed on a Friday in twenty years.
- Episodes:
  1. Gianna sits the learner down and feeds them. They know Emma? "The girl with the birthday! She eats like a bird."
     Amira delivers the saffron and is told off for the price, fondly.
  2. **Emma visits.** Gianna shows her the card. Emma knows the handwriting at once. The recipe on the card has one
     thing the book does not (a spoon of olive oil). They will bake it together.
  3. Gianna and Jonah, who plays in the square outside. She is thinking of closing on a Friday, for the first time in
     twenty years, to go to Emma's Friday dinner in the town.

### Iris, programmer of the Lumen cinema on Arts Street [cinema]
- Zoe in the town runs the cinema and has made a short film; Max is her boyfriend (unless the learner's own story
  changed that: never mention Max and Zoe as a couple in the city's lines, say "her friend Max" at most).
- Iris chooses the films for the Lumen and for its short film week. Blunt to the point of rude, never unfair.
  Zoe's film is in the pile of entries.
- **Wren** sells the tickets and loves Zoe's film more than Iris admits to.
- Episodes:
  1. Iris at the door of the Lumen. No spoilers, no talking in screen two. Wren cannot stop talking about one short
     film from "some little town down the coast". Iris has not decided.
  2. **Zoe comes to ask.** Iris gives her notes: the ending explains too much; the first minute is perfect. Zoe is
     hurt, then fired up.
  3. Iris and Wren put the programme up. Zoe's film is in, nine o'clock on the Friday. Iris admits, to Wren only,
     that she cried at minute seven.

### Claire, editor at Harbour Press and its bookshop on Arts Street (Daniel's sister) [school]
- Daniel in the town teaches, runs an evening class and a conversation club, and has been writing a novel for twelve
  years: "Nobody's read a single page. Not even my sister." "My sister says hello, by the way. She's heard all about
  you." Claire is that sister, a little older.
- She reads other people's books for a living and has never read her brother's. Sad about it more than angry.
- Episodes:
  1. The learner browses. Claire asks what they read, then how they know the town. Daniel's class! "Is he still
     writing it?" Twelve years. "Not a page. Not even me."
  2. **Daniel visits** with the first chapter in his bag. He does not hand it over; she does not ask. They talk about
     commas. When he has gone the envelope is on the counter.
  3. Claire and Kit from Bay FM (he buys his paperbacks from her). She read it in a night. Three pages of notes. If
     she is honest he may stop; she decides honesty is the respect he is owed.

Threads that run between people (whoever writes a line about one keeps to this):

- **The blue scarf** in Pete's lost property is Grace's: she left it on the no. 9 two winters ago and gave it up for
  lost. Pete does not know whose it is; it is only found out when Grace herself is met in Westbay (`cv-home-1`).
- **Tess's summer at Emma's**: Emma taught her that "the milk tells you when it's ready"; Tess still says it.
- **Marcus keeps Tom's letters** in a shoebox under the desk at the studio (Kit has seen it).
- **Jonah's unfinished song** is about the band he and Mia had; Mia has not heard it.
- **Hal** pays the town's anglers by the kilo and complains they only ever bring him small ones.
- **Dot** never comes to the front of the bakery; she shouts from the back, usually one short line.

Heard only: **Dot** (Poppy's twin), **Marcus** (Bay FM), the station's loudspeaker, the bus's loudspeaker.

## 4. Town residents in Westbay

They come up on their own days (`src/core/town/city.ts` CITY_VISITS) and whenever a story needs them:
Sarah (Monday: the weekly meeting at head office), Ben (Tuesday: his dad), Daniel (Wednesday: the bookshop), Grace
(Wednesday: she volunteers at the hospital), Mia (Thursday: karaoke), Emma (Thursday: the market, for Friday's dinner),
Zoe and Max (Friday: short films at the Lumen), Leo (Saturday: his sister and niece), Kim (Saturday: a course at the
hospital), Tom (Sunday: Marcus's show), Lily (weekdays: she works in the city), Sam (any day: the no. 9 is his line).

Facts of the town that the city's lines lean on (all already said in the town):
Grace was a nurse in London for forty years. Max is Australian; his mum wants him to move back to Brisbane. Kim nearly
quit in her first year. Tom moved every year as a kid and hates speaking in public. Sarah has been eight years at the
same company and goes up to the city for meetings. Lily has a new job and stress at work. Sam has a dog, Chip.

## 5. Rules for every line

- Natural spoken English as these people would really say it, 3–16 words a line, contractions and everyday reductions
  welcome (the decoder shows them: gonna, wanna, kind of, a lot of, what did you…). No slang a learner will never meet.
- The learner is there and is talked to, but never speaks.
- Vietnamese meanings: natural, concise, how a Vietnamese person would say it, no English words except names.
- A question must be answerable only by having understood the English; wrong options are plausible and are what a
  half-listening ear would guess. Never ask what a title or the scene line gave away.
- Nothing contradicts §2–§4. A new fact is welcome when it deepens a tie; it then becomes canon: add it here.
- No promise about the learner's level, no "you speak so well": people talk about their lives.

## 6. Facts the scenes added (now canon)

Written into the scenes of `src/data/city/` and `src/data/scenes/rides.ts`; later lines keep to them.

- **The no. 9** passes Old Bridge and Hill Farm; when the city runs its marathon it stops on Bay Street by the
  seafront instead of the station. The town's anglers send their catch up in a cool box on the first bus.
- **Mrs Okafor**: 22 years at the ticket window; has never taken the no. 9 or seen Sam's station, would like to sit on
  his bench and meet Chip, will not ride in Rico's taxi. The loudspeaker asks "the driver in bay three" to move twice a
  day, at her request.
- **Pete**: the rule is three months, then the charity shop; he has logged the blue scarf as part of his uniform to
  keep it. A goldfish was once handed in; Mrs Okafor named it Timetable. He guesses, wrongly, that the scarf is a
  student's.
- **Tess**: worked at Emma's at seventeen, burnt the milk for two weeks; is saving for a real café "with a lemon cake
  like Emma's" (she has tried eleven times; Emma never gave her the recipe, only a slice for the bus).
- **Theo**: walks Maya in laps round the fountain (Hal counts them); goes back to night shifts on the ferries next
  month; is meant to be off sugar. Leo made Maya a lantern that hangs over her cot.
- **Poppy and Dot**: up at four; Gianna's twelve loaves go out first and she sends back any she does not like, with a
  note. Frank has had a cheese roll there every morning for six years. Dot has come to the front once in eighteen
  years (a tea-towel fire) and has never been to the town. The flat above the bakery, the one Nora offers Leo, is
  theirs; Nora asked Poppy to hold it one more week.
- **Hal**: gets seasick; his dad fished the bay for forty years. Sells the town's small fish as crispy starters; last
  week's 4 kg sea bass went in the window with the angler's name on a card.
- **Amira**: came to Nora's school from another country at eleven; Nora sat beside her and ate half her lunch. Their
  teacher was Mrs Long. Amira still counts on her fingers.
- **Jonah and Mia**: the band played twelve gigs, nine in Mia's garage; it split eight years ago and the song was
  started that week. Song forty-two, which Mia sings at Bo's, was its first song. Gianna pays Jonah in pasta. He keeps
  Thursdays free.
- **Wren**: has the programme of Zoe's town cinema on her wall and has never met her; is writing a script on the back
  of old ticket rolls; Iris will read ten pages once it has an ending.
- **Kit and Marcus**: Marcus reads one of Tom's letters before every show; Kit has promised never to tell Tom about
  the shoebox. Kit asked Claire onto the show to talk about three books ("put me down in pencil").
- **Bo**: twelve years of the bar, has never sung in it, wants a live-music night. **Dana** books the big room for
  seven every Thursday; Mia works from home and Thursday is her office day; she always takes the 10:15 back.
