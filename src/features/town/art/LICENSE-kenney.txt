Kenney Mini Characters (2.0), rendered into the sprites in characters/ by scripts/town-art (npm run art:town).
Kenney City Kit (Suburban) 1.0 and City Kit (Roads) 1.1, rendered (with shapes in the same style) into world/.
Only rendered pictures are in this repository; the 3D source models are downloaded by hand into art-src/kenney/.
